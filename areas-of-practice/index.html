Our services

Auditing

Examination of books of account, financial statements and supporting records to assess whether the figures are supported and consistently reported. The firm reviews transactions, identifies discrepancies and communicates findings within the agreed scope of the audit.

Tax consultancy

Practical guidance on tax obligations and the tax implications of income, expenses and business transactions. Advice is based on each client’s circumstances, helping clarify available deductions, documentation requirements and filing responsibilities.

Income tax returns

Preparation and filing of income tax returns using income details, eligible deductions and tax-payment records. The firm checks the information supplied, highlights missing details and prepares the return for the client’s review before submission.

GST

Assistance with preparing GST returns from sales, purchase and tax records. Work includes organising invoice data, reviewing reported figures and identifying mismatches or missing information so that the return reflects the business’s records.

Book-keeping

Recording and organising day-to-day sales, purchases, receipts and payments. Ledgers and supporting documents are maintained in a consistent format, with bank balances checked against records to support accounts preparation and tax filings.

Company compliance

Support with routine company filings and the records needed to prepare them. The firm helps organise company and financial information, prepare the required documentation and track the filing tasks applicable to the engagement.

Registration

Assistance with registration applications, from identifying the appropriate registration to preparing the required details and supporting documents. The firm helps check application information and address documentation queries during the registration process.
